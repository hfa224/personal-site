The Stuttgart Stained Glass window in St David's Hall, Cardiff

My immediate thought for this prompt was stained glass, particularly more contemporary abstract or secular stained glass. I became curious about a piece I remembered seeing during a gig at St David's Hall online and thought I'd create a page about it. Hover and click over areas of the image to learn more.

The band Adwaith, setting up for a gig. The band are a three piece from Camarthen, Wales (only two members are pictured here, on the left and right of the stage). The band's name is a Welsh word meaning reaction, and the band sing almost entirely in Welsh. Below is a song from their first album.

St David's Hall is the National Concert Hall and Conference Centre of Wales, built in 1982 in a brutalist style. There are The greater height of the third floor is exploited in a full-height stained glass window comprising a series of angled panes dominating the eastern elevation behind the stage. It was designed by Hans Gottfried von Stockhausen, and was presented to the city of Cardiff by the people of Stuttgart as a symbol of friendship between the two cities. It was unveiled in 1985, and depicts a representation of the Welsh landscape with cultural references including a rugby post.
