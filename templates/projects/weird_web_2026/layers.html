Sometimes, layers can be chickens, often in cryptic crosswords.

What is a cryptic crossword?

Cryptic Crosswords are a type of crossword where the clue is written as wordplay, rather than a "stright" definition. e.g., a crossword clue for "Dog" may be: "Male sheep (3)", and a cryptic clue may be "To scar in reverse for butter (3)".

The cryptic clue contains 2 parts: a defintion (usually trickier than a regular clue), and wordplay which enables you to solve for the answer independently of the definition (in this case, To scar (mar) reversed gives Ram, the solution -> an animal that butts).

Cryptic crosswords have quite formal rules for the type of wordplay which is allowed which can be inscrutable to a beginner. I learnt to do them at work through doing the Guardian cryptic every lunchtime with my colleagues.

Here's a list of some other alternate definitions:

Is a flower a flower 🌺, or a flower 🌊?

Is lower to lower ⬇️, or a lower 🐄?

Is a butter butter 🧈, or a butter 🐏?

Is a shower a shower 🚿, or a shower 📺?

Is close to be close, or to close?

Is detail to detail, or to de-tail?
